# Neon Champions Product Constitution

## Purpose

This is the short north-star contract for the four-week proof, eight-week prototype, and six-month launch-capable candidate. Detailed GDDs may expand it but may not contradict it without human approval.

## Player promise

Lead a public cyberpunk Champion across a frozen frontier, explore and control strategic infrastructure, build a faction base, shape an army, fight readable tactical battles, and contest what people believe happened beneath the engineered White Sky.

## Audience and format

- Single-player-first turn-based strategy/RPG for players who value HoMM-like exploration, faction identity, base anticipation, tactical battles, and campaign consequence.
- HRC and QXZ are both playable and morally non-binary. The proof scenario has no canonical hero faction.
- No direct magic; fantasy functions are translated into command, biotech, drones, institutions, infrastructure, information, and public legitimacy.

## Pillars

1. Strategic clarity under dirty information.
2. Infrastructure is power.
3. Champions are command identities and public symbols.
4. Factions express different evolutionary philosophies through incentives, not recolors.
5. Arctic wilderness creates distance, pressure, and dependence rather than empty scenery.
6. Intel turns secrets and proof into concrete power.

## Anti-pillars

- No debug-dashboard player experience.
- No generic nation-versus-corporation war or morally simple faction split.
- No administrative meter pile that obscures movement, construction, recruitment, combat, and objectives.
- No content breadth purchased with shallow factions or unreadable battles.
- No agent-authored canon or design drift.

## Core loop

Read map and pressure -> choose route and commitment -> move/interact -> construct/recruit/contest -> fight tactically when engaged -> gain or lose resources, Intel, infrastructure, and public consequence -> react to rival and scenario pressure.

## Delivery gates

- Four weeks: presentable 20–30 minute proof showing exploration, base construction, tactical combat, and one Feed consequence.
- Eight weeks: complete 60–90 minute prototype with AI, save/resume, onboarding, internal scenario authoring, outside tests, and clear differentiation.
- Six months: launch-capable Early Access candidate followed by evidence-based launch/no-launch review.

## Product rule

Every prototype feature must produce a player verb, an immediate readable consequence, opponent counterplay where applicable, and a place in the core loop. Systems that cannot meet this test remain event framing or deferred research.

## Approval gate

Pending human approval as the north star. Approval authorizes reconciliation of `game-concept.md` and `game-pillars.md`; it does not approve final campaign canon, final balance, final region name, or post-prototype feature depth.
