# HRC and QXZ Prototype Faction Contracts

## Scope

Human-approved provisional contract for the four-week proof: six possible facilities and three tactical lines per faction. HRC/QXZ identities, facility and unit names, exact values, tactical emphasis, and presentation may be tuned through implementation and playtesting without reopening the faction-pair decision. Material changes to moral framing or core strategic identity still require human approval.

## Home Rule Coalition

Player fantasy: defend local autonomy through adaptation, trusted networks, rescue capacity, and terrain knowledge.

Strategic advantage: flexible low-cost adaptation and benefits from civic/local infrastructure.
Strategic liability: weaker centralized scaling and less efficient conversion into elite force.
Tactical rhythm: mobile defense, marking, sustain, and objective rescue.
Information relationship: witnesses, local mesh, and recovered proof.
Infrastructure relationship: repair, reuse, distribute, and protect.

### Six-facility prototype tree

| Facility | Function | Player decision | Contrast |
|---|---|---|---|
| Council Office | Credits/administration | steady broad local income | distributed, modest scaling |
| Repair Cooperative | Materials conversion/repair | preserve tempo or stockpile | reuse rather than industrial throughput |
| Watch Muster | durable/control recruitment | secure positions and objectives | accessible civic defense |
| Scout Relay | mobile/recon recruitment | reveal and mark routes/targets | local knowledge |
| Witness Mesh | Intel/proof | verify a lead or gain local visibility | trust/provenance |
| Operations Garage | Champion support | mobility, recovery, or special project access | improvised field capability |

### Three tactical lines

1. Settlement Watch / Council Guard — durable control; protects objectives and adjacent allies.
2. Hunter-Scouts / Fjord Stalkers — mobile aggression/recon; marks targets and exploits terrain.
3. SAR Drone Crew / Rescue Mesh Wing — ranged information/support; recovery, reveal, and reposition support.

## QXZ Meridian Arctic Mandate

Player fantasy: impose reliable climate stability through proprietary systems, forecasting, and disciplined specialization.

Strategic advantage: efficient specialization, prediction, and infrastructure protection.
Strategic liability: high costs, dependency on controlled systems, and visible legitimacy exposure.
Tactical rhythm: forecast, establish control, then execute with protected combined arms.
Information relationship: predictive models, sensors, and liability-grade records.
Infrastructure relationship: stabilize, instrument, own, and enforce.

### Six-facility prototype tree

| Facility | Function | Player decision | Contrast |
|---|---|---|---|
| Concession Office | Credits/administration | concentrated income with dependency | efficient central scaling |
| Climate Fabricator | Materials conversion/repair | accelerate build or maintain systems | clean industrial throughput |
| Mandate Barracks | durable/control recruitment | disciplined baseline force | costly reliability |
| Stratospheric Lab | specialist/recon recruitment | sensors and forecast advantage | technical specialization |
| Risk Analytics Cell | Intel/information | preview threat or improve verification | model authority |
| Continuity Suite | Champion support | protection, recovery, or protocol project | controlled premium capability |

### Three tactical lines

1. Meridian Security / Mandate Response Team — durable control; disciplined objective protection.
2. Aerosol Techs / Skyline Engineers — mobile/aggressive utility; deploys or repairs battlefield control.
3. Climate Bulwark / Albedo Bastion — ranged/control support; shielding, denial, and infrastructure protection.

## Shared prototype rules

- One construction decision per base per strategic cycle.
- Six facilities are available; only about three or four should be affordable in the proof.
- Facilities change options or tempo, not only passive percentages.
- Unit lines must remain readable at strategic, engagement-preview, and tactical scales.
- Upgrade/loadout branches are deferred until the base line is legible and useful.
- Exact costs, prerequisites, stock refresh, and Champion bonuses require a separate tuning packet.

## Acceptance questions

1. Do both factions create different opening/build-order decisions?
2. Can a player describe the faction from behavior without names/colors?
3. Does every facility visibly change a decision in the short scenario?
4. Do the three unit lines support more than one viable composition?
