# Approved World Slice

Game-facing lore approved for design use will be summarized here.

## Current accepted anchors

- Neon Champions is cyberpunk / post-cyberpunk strategy-RPG territory, inspired by HoMM3 but not a clone.
- Single-player is primary.
- No direct magic.
- White Sky is literal albedo/geoengineering and has strategic consequences.
- Factions should be philosophies of evolution.
- Champions are contested commander/identity figures, not simple superheroes.
- Digital-Net / polluted feeds can support fog-of-war, provenance, misinformation, and public legitimacy systems.
- States remain recognizable, but lived power is layered through maintenance, concessions, climate service, compute/energy priority, and information/provenance systems.
