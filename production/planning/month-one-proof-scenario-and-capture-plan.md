# Month-One Proof Scenario and Capture Plan

## Proof question

Can Neon Champions communicate its complete identity—exploration, base choice, faction composition, tactical conflict, and contested public truth—in a coherent 20–30 minute slice?

## Scenario frame

A contested White Sky sensor/calibration station near a fishery and airstrip begins emitting contradictory failure reports during Blue Monday. HRC and QXZ each claim legitimate emergency authority. The player chooses either faction; neither path is canonical or morally clean.

## Working map budget

- 10–14 meaningful locations, one base per faction.
- Three viable opening routes.
- Resource cluster, recruitment opportunity, Intel/proof site, optional high-risk prize, tactical objective, and final linked objective.
- Rival pressure visible by cycle 2–3.
- One costly early commitment and one information-driven route reversal.

## Required beats

1. Faction choice and concise briefing.
2. First route choice with visible tradeoff.
3. Base construction choice among six possibilities, with only three or four affordable over the slice.
4. Recruitment/composition decision.
5. Site contest leading to readable tactical battle.
6. Intel/proof discovery that changes understanding or access.
7. Feed consequence presenting competing interpretation.
8. Linked victory/defeat resolution.

## Capture shot list

1. Hero map shot: White Sky, routes, base, Champion, and objective pressure.
2. HRC base construction choice with visible structural identity.
3. QXZ base construction choice with contrasting identity.
4. Army/Champion selection showing composition clearly.
5. Tactical wide shot with readable factions, terrain, objective, and intent.
6. Close action shot with forecast and result feedback.
7. Feed reveal showing provenance/contested truth without a text wall.
8. Victory/defeat frame with consequence and next-question hook.

Video target: one coherent 30–60 second sequence assembled from real gameplay, not camera-only mockups.

## Gate metrics

Five-second test for each screenshot; unaided task-completion test for start/move/build/recruit/fight/end; tester can name both faction differences and two game-specific differentiators; no critical action depends on developer explanation.

## Deferred

Final region name, full campaign spine, save/load, full tutorial, public editor, multiplayer, final art, full rosters, and public downloadable build.
